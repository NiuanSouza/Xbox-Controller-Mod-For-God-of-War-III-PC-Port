================================================================================
INSTALLATION (DROP-IN FOLDER)
================================================================================

No installation scripts, installers, or batch files are required. Installing is as simple as placing this folder into your mods directory:

1. Copy the EXEC folder from this directory:
   /3_Xbox_Official_Series/EXEC
2. Paste it into your game's active mod directory:
   mods/
   (Or simply copy the entire 3_Xbox_Official_Series folder into mods/)

3. Launch the game normally via "Play God of War III.exe" or "God of War III.exe"!
   The PC port natively overlays modded WAD files into memory at startup without modifying your original PS4 dump.
